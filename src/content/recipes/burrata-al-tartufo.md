---
title: Burrata al Tartufo
img: https://ik.imagekit.io/g6ujpydkwn/gustoso/recipes/burrata-al-tartufo_wgdyXddRf.jpg
type: Starter
serves: 1 portion
parts:
  - name: Assembly and plating
    ingredients:
      - 100 g basil-roasted tomatoes (1 GREEN ladle)
      - 5 ml basil oil
      - 2 focaccia slivers (one third of a portion), oiled and grilled
      - 30 g mushroom and truffle purée (20 g for focaccia, 10 g for burrata)
      - 1 burrata, drained (specified in the method, omitted from ingredient
        table)
      - 2.5 ml extra-virgin olive oil
      - 1 pinch Maldon sea salt
      - 1 pinch cracked black pepper
      - 5–6 pea-shoot sprigs
    instructions:
      - step: 1
        description: Put the tomatoes in the centre of a cold pasta bowl and add the
          basil oil.
      - step: 2
        description: Spread 10 g mushroom and truffle purée on each focaccia sliver and
          arrange with the tomatoes.
      - step: 3
        description: Top the tomatoes with the drained burrata and make an incision
          across it.
      - step: 4
        description: Top the burrata with the remaining 10 g purée. Add salt, pepper,
          pea shoots, and the extra-virgin olive oil.
---
