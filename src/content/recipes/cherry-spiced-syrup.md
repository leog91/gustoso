---
title: Cherry Spiced Syrup
type: Syrup
serves: 400 g
parts:
  - name: Cherry spiced syrup
    ingredients:
      - 1 cinnamon stick
      - 5 whole cloves
      - 5 whole black peppercorns
      - Peel of 1 orange
      - 250 g caster sugar
      - 250 g water
      - 75 g Luxardo cherry juice
    instructions:
      - step: 1
        description: Put the cinnamon, cloves, peppercorns, orange peel, sugar, and water in a pan and bring to the boil. Reserve the cherry juice for after cooling.
      - step: 2
        description: Continue boiling until the syrup reaches 106°C, approximately 6–7 minutes. Measure with a probe.
      - step: 3
        description: Immediately remove from the heat and decant into a clean metal container.
      - step: 4
        description: Once cooled, strain out the cinnamon, peppercorns, cloves, and peel. Add the cherry juice.
    storage: 3 days.
---
