title: Panettone Trifle
type: Dessert
parts:
  - name: Zabaione pâtissière — source batch yield 1 kg
    ingredients:
      - 750 ml milk
      - 6 egg yolks
      - 150 g caster sugar
      - 1/2 vanilla pod, split
      - 120 g flour
      - 80 g Marsala wine
    instructions:
      - step: 1
        description: Bring the milk to a simmer with the vanilla pod.
      - step: 2
        description: Whisk the egg yolks and sugar until pale and thickened, then whisk in the flour.
      - step: 3
        description: Slowly mix the warm milk into the egg-yolk mixture.
      - step: 4
        description: Return to the pan and heat gently until thickened. Remove from the heat and transfer to a mixing bowl.
      - step: 5
        description: Add the Marsala and mix together. Chill and store in a piping bag.
  - name: Cherry spiced syrup — source batch yield 400 g
    ingredients:
      - 1 cinnamon stick
      - 5 whole cloves
      - 5 whole black peppercorns
      - Peel of 1 orange
      - 250 g caster sugar
      - 250 g water
      - 75 g Luxardo cherry juice (source ingredient row says cherries, juice only)
    instructions:
      - step: 1
        description: Put the cinnamon, cloves, peppercorns, orange peel, sugar, and water in a pan and bring to the boil. Reserve the cherry juice for after cooling.
      - step: 2
        description: Continue boiling until the syrup reaches 106°C, approximately 6–7 minutes. Measure with a probe.
      - step: 3
        description: Immediately remove from the heat and decant into a clean metal container.
      - step: 4
        description: Once cooled, strain out the cinnamon, peppercorns, cloves, and peel. Add the cherry juice.
  - name: Trifle assembly
    ingredients:
      - Panettone
      - Zabaione pâtissière
      - Cherry spiced syrup
      - Luxardo cherries, halved
    instructions:
      - step: 1
        description: Tear the panettone into bite-sized pieces and put a layer in a glass or serving container.
      - step: 2
        description: Spoon over some zabaione pâtissière and add a few cherries.
      - step: 3
        description: Repeat the layers of panettone and zabaione pâtissière as desired.
      - step: 4
        description: Top with cherries and drizzle with cherry spiced syrup.
