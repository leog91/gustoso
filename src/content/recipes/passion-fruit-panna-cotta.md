title: Passion Fruit Panna Cotta
type: Dessert
parts:
  - name: Passion fruit panna cotta
    ingredients:
      - 4 gelatine leaves
      - 900 ml double cream
      - 150 ml whole milk
      - 110 g caster sugar
      - 300 g passion fruit purée, defrosted
    instructions:
      - step: 1
        description: Soften the gelatine leaves in cold water.
      - step: 2
        description: Put the milk, cream, and passion fruit purée in a saucepan. Heat
          the mixture but DO NOT BOIL.
      - step: 3
        description: Remove from the heat, add the sugar, and stir until dissolved.
      - step: 4
        description: Squeeze the excess water from the gelatine and stir it into the
          warm mixture until dissolved.
      - step: 5
        description: Pour into moulds, leaving 1 cm at the top. Blast chill covered,
          then store chilled.
    storage: Store chilled.
  - name: Mango and passion fruit syrup
    ingredients:
      - 150 g caster sugar
      - 150 g water
      - 2 passion fruit, flesh and seeds removed
      - 1/2 mango, peeled and finely diced
      - Juice of 1 lemon
    instructions:
      - step: 1
        description: Peel and finely dice the half mango, remove the flesh and seeds
          from the passion fruit, and juice the lemon.
      - step: 2
        description: Combine the water, sugar, and passion fruit flesh and seeds in a
          saucepan. Bring to the boil.
      - step: 3
        description: When the syrup reaches 106°C, after approximately 5–8 minutes,
          remove from the heat and decant.
      - step: 4
        description: Once cooled, add the lemon juice.
      - step: 5
        description: Add the diced mango and store.
    storage: 3 days
  - name: Plating — one portion
    ingredients:
      - 1 prepared passion fruit panna cotta
      - 15 ml mango and passion fruit syrup
      - 15 ml crumbled Cantucci biscuit
      - 1 sprig mint
    instructions:
      - step: 1
        description: Unmould the panna cotta.
      - step: 2
        description: Spoon the syrup on top and to the side.
      - step: 3
        description: Garnish with the crumbled Cantucci biscuit and mint.
